ignis, ignis, m. - fire

nomen, nominis, n. - name
vis, -, f. - force, strength
vires, virium, f. pl. - force, strength

nos - we
vos - you (pl.)

noster, nostra, nostrum - our
similis, simile + genitive or dative - like
tantus, tanta, tantum - so great
tuus, tua, tuum - your, yours (pl.)
vester, vestra, vestrum - your, yours (pl.)

**VERBS**
consumo, consumere, consumpsi, consumptum - to consume
facio, facere, feci, factum - to do, make
inquit - s/he says or said (note that in classical Latin this verb is only used with direct speech)
occido, occidere, occidi, occisum - to kill
ostendo, ostendere, ostendi, ostentum - to show
pono, ponere, posui, positum - to put, place
