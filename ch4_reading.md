fugit – flees
gerit – wages
inquit – says
intrō, intrāre, intrāvī, ____ – to enter

**ADVERBS**

**PREPOSITIONS**
ad + accusative – into, towards, to
ē (ex before a vowel) + ablative – from, out of
in + accusative – into, to

**CONJUNCTIONS**

sī – if

**PHRASES**
